# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

RenderBlox exists to make Roblox animation **easy for everyone** — especially kids who don't have a good enough PC to run Blender, or who are just learning what "keyframe" means. That goal only works if the community around the tool is **welcoming, safe, and boring in the good way**.

So here it is, spelled out plainly.

## The short version

**Be cool. Don't be a creep. Don't post anything you wouldn't show your grandma or your little brother.**

## What RenderBlox is for

RenderBlox is a **kid-friendly, browser-based alternative to Blender** for making Roblox R6 animations. It exists so that people who can't install a 500 MB 3D suite — or who can't afford a gaming PC, or who are still learning — can still make the animations they want to make.

That purpose isn't a restriction on what you can *do* with the software. You can make whatever you want with it, and the code is MIT-licensed so you can fork it, modify it, and ship your own version. But **the boundaries in this document apply to anything you do in this repo and its community spaces**. The tool is free to be used; the community around it is not free to be poisoned.

You can make a horror animation. You can make an edgy one. You can make a weird one. But you cannot make a sexual one, you cannot make one that targets real people with hate, and you cannot use this project's spaces to harass people. Those aren't restrictions on the software — those are restrictions on *you, here, in this repo.*

## The rules

### Absolutely not allowed

- ❌ **Sexual or romantic animations** — no ships, no kissing, no NSFW anything
- ❌ **NSFW imagery** — no nudity, no suggestive costumes, no "funny" anatomy
- ❌ **Gore or realistic violence** — cartoon bonks are fine; dismembered-body horror is not
- ❌ **Hate speech** — no slurs, no targeting people for race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, or anything else
- ❌ **Harassment** — no bullying, no dogpiling, no stalking
- ❌ **Doxxing** — no sharing someone's real name, address, workplace, or face without consent
- ❌ **Spam or self-promotion disguised as content** — no "watch my 4-hour stream" or "buy my course"
- ❌ **Content promoting self-harm** — no suicide jokes, no eating disorder content, no "kys" and no "you should die"
- ❌ **Anything illegal** — no piracy links, no credential sharing, no exploit scripts

### Fine but be thoughtful

- ⚠️ **Cartoon violence** — bonks, explosions, goofy slapstick is fine. Repeated gore is not.
- ⚠️ **Mild profanity** — the occasional "damn" is fine. Slurs are not.
- ⚠️ **Edgy humour** — depends on context. Ask yourself "would a 10-year-old get this and be okay with it?"
- ⚠️ **Criticism** — of the tool, of the code, of the design is welcome. Of people is not.

### Encouraged

- ✅ **Share your animations** — the whole point of the tool is to make cool stuff
- ✅ **Report bugs** — every bug fixed makes the tool better for everyone
- ✅ **Help beginners** — the majority of the user base is under 16
- ✅ **Contribute code** — features, fixes, docs, all welcome
- ✅ **Give feedback** — politely, specifically, and with reproduction steps

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- The RenderBlox source repository — issues, PRs, commit messages, code comments
- Anywhere you're representing RenderBlox
- Anywhere the maintainer is present as a RenderBlox maintainer

If you're not sure whether your content is allowed, **ask first** via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/renderfox5213/renderblox/issues). Better to ask than to be removed.

## What happens if you break the rules

Different violations get different responses.

| Severity | Example | Response |
|----------|---------|----------|
| Low | Mildly rude comment | Private warning from a maintainer |
| Medium | Repeated rudeness, edgy jokes | Temporary block from the repository (1–7 days) |
| High | Sexual content, hate speech | Permanent block, issue/PR closed |
| Critical | Doxxing, illegal content | Permanent block + report to GitHub Trust & Safety |

Moderators can skip straight to a permanent block if the violation is severe enough. There is no "three strikes" rule — we're not a baseball game.

## Reporting

If you see content or behaviour that breaks this Code of Conduct:

1. **Do not engage.** Do not quote, do not reply, do not "just this once" engage with the offender.
2. **Report it.** Use GitHub's report button on the specific comment/issue, or open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/renderfox5213/renderblox/security/advisories/new) if the situation is sensitive.
3. **Include context.** Screenshots, links, timestamps — whatever helps the maintainer understand what happened.
4. **Wait.** Reports will be handled within a few days. Do not repeatedly re-report the same incident.

**Reports are confidential.** The maintainer will not reveal who reported what, and retaliation against reporters is itself a permanent-block violation.

## The "I was just joking" defence

Not a defence. Jokes about sexual assault, slurs, or suicide are not jokes. If you have to add "just kidding" to it, the joke isn't funny to the person it's hurting.

## The "but Roblox has worse content" defence

Not a defence. Roblox is a platform with millions of users and a team of full-time moderators. RenderBlox is one developer with a GitHub repo. We hold a higher standard because we can, and because the tool is used by kids.

## The "free speech" defence

Not a defence. RenderBlox is a private project on private infrastructure. Freedom of speech means the government can't arrest you for saying things — it doesn't mean the maintainer of a Roblox animation tool has to host your content on their GitHub repository.

## The "it's open source, I can do what I want" defence

You can **fork** RenderBlox and do whatever you want with the code — that's what MIT means. But "open source" does not mean "this repo's issue tracker is your personal soapbox." The rules above apply to this community, in this repo, on these issues. If you want to make content that breaks those rules, that's your right — but do it elsewhere, with your own fork, under your own name. Don't drag the project into it.

## Amendments

This Code of Conduct can be updated at any time. The current version is always the one in the `main` branch of the repository. Significant changes will be noted in the commit message when they land.
